# Form 3 Biology and Physics content scope

**Status:** Draft content for a pilot. It has not been approved by the Malawi Institute of Education or reviewed by a local teacher. Do not describe it as officially syllabus-aligned until that review is completed.

## Topic outline used for the first pack

The topic headings below are provisional and taken from a Malawi Form 3 overview page that labels itself “Under development.” They are used to organize a starter learning pack, not as proof of the current official syllabus sequence.

- **Biology:** Cells; Movement in and out of cells; Plant structure; Photosynthesis; Transport in plants; Ecosystem basics.
- **Physics:** Measurements II; Scientific Investigations; Kinetic theory of matter; Thermometry; Pressure; Gas Laws; Scalar and vector quantities.

The lesson explanations and practice questions are original draft learning material based on standard introductory science concepts. Each pack contains one short lesson and two practice questions per listed topic. A biology or physics teacher in Malawi should check topic coverage, terminology, grade level, calculations, and assessment expectations before classroom use.

## Sources and limitations

The Malawi Institute of Education (MIE) downloads page is the official source reviewed for this implementation. Its extracted page listed an updated 2024 secondary-school book list and other institutional downloads, but it did not expose the Form 3 Biology or Physics syllabus documents used to verify topic sequence: [MIE Downloads](https://mie.edu.mw/downloads/).

The topic headings were checked against [Malawi Curriculum Online — Form 3](https://maryqueenofpeace.africa/curriculum_online/form-3/), which explicitly says the page is under development. Treat it as a provisional overview, not an official or final curriculum authority.

[WTEC1 LMS — JCE & MSCE Syllabuses](https://edu.wtec1.com/pdfs/) was also inspected as a third-party syllabus catalogue. It describes MSCE as Forms 3–4 but the individual Biology and Physics syllabus files were not verified for this starter pack.

Before expanding coverage, obtain the current official Biology and Physics syllabus documents from MIE or the Ministry of Education, confirm any curriculum transition, and have a local subject teacher approve the resulting content and questions. Content-pack metadata keeps the review status visible in the app until then.
